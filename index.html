History of AI

Google didn't invent attention. Bahdanau, Cho and Bengio introduced it in 2014, lifting a translation model's score from 17.8 to 26.8 BLEU, and others refined it into self-attention by 2016. Google's 2017 Transformer took the last step: attention alone, with no recurrence.
